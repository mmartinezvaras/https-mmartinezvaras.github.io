# Matching Strategies

## How to Map JD Requirements to CV Experience

### Step 1: Categorize Requirements

From the parsed JD, categorize each requirement:

| Category | Examples | Weight |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **Core** | "10+ years in demand gen", "proven pipeline track record" | Must address |
| **Technical** | "Salesforce", "SQL", "marketing automation" | Match or flag |
| **Soft/Leadership** | "cross-functional collaboration", "team building" | Show through examples |
| **Nice-to-have** | "experience in AI/ML", "MBA preferred" | Bonus, don't stretch |

### Step 2: Score Each Match

For each requirement, find the best match in the CV and score it:

- **90-100% (Direct)** – CV explicitly states this. Use as-is, add JD keywords.
- **60-89% (Adjacent)** – Related experience that can be reframed. Emphasize the relevant angle.
- **30-59% (Weak)** – Tangential connection. Only use if nothing better exists. Candidate for experience discovery.
- **0-29% (Gap)** – No meaningful connection. Flag honestly.

### Step 3: Reframing Techniques

When a match is adjacent (60-89%), reframe by:

1. **Mirroring language** – Use the JD's exact terminology where honest
   - CV says "managed paid campaigns" → JD says "demand channels" → Reframe: "Identified and scaled high-leverage demand channels including paid search, social, and programmatic"

2. **Shifting emphasis** – Same experience, different angle
   - CV bullet about "built BDR team" → JD wants "scaling programs" → Reframe: "Scaled outbound pipeline by building and managing BDR team from zero"

3. **Adding context** – Make implicit experience explicit
   - CV says "managed enterprise accounts" → JD wants "complex buying groups" → Reframe: "Managed enterprise accounts targeting complex buying groups across C-suite, technical, and operational personas"

### Step 4: Prioritize

- Lead with strongest matches in each section
- Expand bullets for core requirements, compress for non-essential
- If a non-current role has the strongest match for a core requirement, consider giving it more space than usual (but keep chronological order)
- Don't pad weak sections – better to have fewer strong bullets than many weak ones
